⭐ Animal welfare score — how it works

Protein (max 50 pts)
≥50% dry matter = full points; scales down to 0 at 25%.
Fat (max 30 pts)
Ideal band 15–25% dry matter; penalised outside 10–35%.
Carbs (max 20 pts)
Cats need very few: 0–5% = full points; >20% = zero.

➕ Add a wet food macros should be the “analytical constituents” from the label, in %

Tip: if the label only shows protein/fat/fibre/ash/moisture, carbs are estimated as 100 − moisture − protein − fat − fibre − ash (Nitrogen-Free Extract).
